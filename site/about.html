Built on real rooms.

The 256 official rooms and the 3D runtime come from MazeBenchEngine, pinned at commit 0ac96b8a2648db09f375989cd7bc33699222c1e6. The four skill-built rooms are existing agent-authored trials: Shiftlock Vault, Offset Gallery, Borrowed Aisle, and Glacier Turn. All source geometry is preserved.

A static home for the original engine.

The browser uses the original game rules, rigid bodies, ice behavior, and room transitions. The hosting adaptation changes asset URLs, JSON loading, room URLs, and touch-button hit targets, and skips the opening flyover. Twenty official rooms have no authored player; direct jumps provide an arrival actor at an open boundary so those rooms can also be played immediately. Normal doorway travel is unchanged. Source hashes, exported hashes, map origins, and the exact adaptations are recorded in the provenance manifest. The full skill evidence remains in the repository.
