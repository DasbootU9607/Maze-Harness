# Composition

Use another authoring skill when it adds a concrete role. Boundary stagger can change the cross endpoint; constrained transport can prepare docking; a hook can relocate a stop through contact. Explain the shared space, stance or clearance causing the dependency.

Ice changes reachability, distances and contact timing. Other skills' unit-step contact observers do not certify sliding, and this one-group profile does not certify relays. Keep official physics and use compatible observation/restriction checks before claiming a combined pass.

Recheck the complete goal, return, supports, swept clearance, actual contacts, directed stops, preparation and bypasses. A new blocker may remove a brake, turn a stance into a transit square or strand a parking pose. Limit conclusions to the tested map/state scope.
