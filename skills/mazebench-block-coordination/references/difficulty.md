# Reference selection and relative difficulty

Use [reference-catalog.json](reference-catalog.json) to select a compatible relation branch and complete objective. Both ratings are provisional structural estimates, not official rankings or human playtest scores.

| Reference | Tier | Structural comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BxB | l2 Combination | Several initially available independent shields connect to a final tool reuse after a safe-ground turn. |
| BxC | l3 Planning | Common delivery must connect to separate target placement, a clear-ground return, and another block creating the final input stance; more preparation precedes the handoff. |

Block count, room geometry and doorway endpoint differ, so the comparison is not a controlled human-difficulty experiment. Neither route length, search timeout nor the existence of a human recording establishes difficulty. The l1 Foundation tier remains empty and denotes a compact relation with accessible preparation; l2 links preparations/stance changes/reuse; l3 adds deeper dependencies and shared working-space constraints. Ties and revisions are allowed when evidence warrants them. Later references need their own human focus, full ordinary completion and explicitly qualified evidence through the case workflow.

## Basis for the retained ordering

| Dimension | BxB witness | BxC witness | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preparation depth | Next handoff shields are initially placed; progression delivers the required input sides. | The future working shield is placed separately before another tool supplies its input side. | The extra dependency in the observed plan supports the provisional higher tier. It is not proved unavoidable for all BxC solutions. |
| Coordination and reuse | Sequential roles followed by another use of the same final tool. | Shared delivery, separate roles and preservation of a parked tool during approach work. | Different kinds of coordination; joint movement alone is not a difficulty score. |
| Access and recovery | Clear ground supports changing sides around M2. | Clear ground reconnects the player to M1 while M0 stays prepared. | Both require an executable reapproach; neither observation ranks all possible recovery options. |
| Commitment and visibility | Reachable handoff opportunities are observed. | Preparation has a later payoff demonstrated by the paired branch control. | Systematic recovery difficulty and human recognition remain unmeasured. |

The [cross-case comparison](reference-library.md) supplies the supporting events and scope. Keep l2/l3 as selection aids, not quantitative scores. To examine a difficulty factor later, use a separately authorized variant that preserves the entry, objective and core relations while changing one identifiable preparation or access constraint. Merely adding blocks, route length or dead walking does not establish a higher tier. No such variant was created here.
