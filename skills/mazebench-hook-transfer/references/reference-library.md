# Hook Transfer reference synthesis

Use [the catalog](reference-catalog.json) and [difficulty guide](difficulty.md) to choose source functions after reading [mechanism logic](mechanism-logic.md). References supply different realizations and evidence scopes; do not concatenate their routes or copy their coordinates into a new-map contract.

| Case | Contribution | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GxE](official-case.md) | Independent docking, actual rear release, and continued work toward a passage. | Retains its exhausted case-specific exclusions and replayed bypass of a proposed exact continuation. |
| [FxE](official-fxe-case.md) | A released target becomes a tool; a supported span and its foot serve successive contacts across separated input/work areas. | Retains exhausted early exclusions, capped later exclusions and selected states separately. |
| [FxG](official-fxg-case.md) | Shared-space competition, withdrawal that restores a next input, and reassignment of tools across two doorway objectives. | Full uninterrupted two-door replay and controlled selected-state access releases; global role/contact/preparation necessity is unconfirmed. |

The common causal structure is preparation -> reachable input and true member contact -> changed restriction -> executable subsequent work -> complete objective. FxG adds `ht-shared-clearance` and `ht-passage-handoff` as conditional branches. A previously useful tool may obstruct another input, and a released blocker may become the next tool. Each change of role needs its own P/I/C/T, complete footprint, support, collision and recovery analysis. Co-motion alone does not identify the working pair.

Preserve the established authoring order: complete goal -> interaction roles -> actual stances and contacts -> full-body geometry/support/clearance -> layout -> ordinary replay and relevant exclusions. Retain the existing default complex mode and empty new-map template. Neither a reference tier nor a successful source witness removes new-map verification obligations.

GxE/FxE maps, routes, mechanical declarations, case documents and historical evidence remain unchanged. The catalog inventories those pre-existing references with explicitly provisional structural grades, then adds FxG using the supplied relative higher placement. Different goals and entries remain visible rather than being treated as a controlled difficulty comparison.

No new maze was generated for this extension. It establishes additional source-backed design relations and a reproducible official witness; independent new-layout transfer, a unique solution order and global shape minimality remain untested.
